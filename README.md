# Restaurant-Sales-Analysis

Restaurant Sales Analysis

⭐ Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing restaurant data to understand
restaurant distribution, cuisines, cities, pricing, ratings, online
delivery, table booking, cuisine combinations, and customer engagement.
The analysis follows a practical Data Analyst workflow:
Raw Data → Data Cleaning → Exploratory Data Analysis → Business
Analysis → Visualization → Insights

🟡 Situation
A restaurant dataset containing business and customer-related
information was available for analysis.
The dataset included information such as restaurant name, city,
cuisines, average cost for two, table booking, online delivery, price
range, aggregate rating, rating color, and votes.
The challenge was to transform the raw restaurant data into meaningful
business insights.

🔵 Task
The main objectives were to:
- Clean and understand the restaurant dataset.
- Analyze restaurant distribution across cities.
- Identify popular cuisines.
- Analyze cuisine combinations.
- Study restaurant price ranges.
- Analyze online-delivery availability.
- Analyze table-booking availability.
- Examine restaurant ratings.
- Compare votes and ratings.
- Analyze restaurant chains and their performance.
- Explore geographic restaurant distribution.

🟢 Action
The analysis was performed using Python and multiple data-analysis and
visualization libraries.
Key steps included:
- Loaded and inspected the restaurant dataset.
- Examined data types and dataset structure.
- Checked the available restaurant attributes.
- Analyzed restaurant distribution by city.
- Identified the most common cuisines.
- Calculated cuisine percentages.
- Analyzed restaurant price ranges.
- Analyzed online-delivery availability and percentages.
- Compared table-booking availability across price ranges.
- Examined restaurant ratings.
- Analyzed cuisine combinations and restaurant counts.
- Studied restaurant chains and their ratings.
- Compared restaurant votes with ratings.
- Performed geographic analysis using location data.
- Created visualizations to communicate business findings.

🔴 Result
The analysis highlighted several useful restaurant-business patterns:
- Indian cuisine was a major cuisine category in the analysis.
- Restaurant distribution varied significantly across cities.
- Restaurants were distributed across different price ranges.
- Mid-range restaurants showed notable representation.
- Mid-range restaurants offered online delivery most often in the
  analysis.
- Expensive restaurants offered online delivery least often.
- Higher-priced restaurants were more likely to offer table booking.
- Cuisine combinations showed differences in restaurant count and
  ratings.
- Votes provided an additional measure of customer engagement
  alongside ratings.
- Restaurant-chain analysis helped compare restaurant presence and
  ratings.
The project demonstrates how restaurant data can be transformed into
business insights that support customer, pricing, delivery, and
restaurant-performance analysis.
